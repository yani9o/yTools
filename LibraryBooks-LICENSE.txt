Library Books data attribution

Book IDs, quest IDs, location records and English/German location hints adapted
from Archivist for Forever 1.0.13 by Sanscrit, released November 5, 2026.
https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/archivist-for-forever
https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/archivist-for-forever/files/9462982

books, alternate pickup locations and cave entrance hints. No custom artwork
